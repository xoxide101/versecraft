# VerseCraft

Walk into any story: a browser game engine that turns places, people and stories into worlds you can explore.
The first world, **RealmCraft: Wrenhaven Town & Harbor**, is a living harbour town:
- **Buildings:** aged and weathered.
- **Land and water:** a brook with bridges, piers with moored boats.
- **People:** townsfolk with jobs and daily routes.
- **Time and weather:** a day and night, weather that builds and eases, seasons.

- **Play:** open `realmcraft/index.html` in Chrome or Edge (no server, no install). WASD to move, Space to talk or use, drag to turn the view, wheel to zoom, T for time, G for weather.
